E.01 · If only a weekend is available

Four sources that cover most of this properly

R.01

Nathan Lambert, Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

Free at rlhfbook.com; also arXiv:2504.12501; print edition from Manning, 2026.

The single best source for chapter 02. Chapters 4 to 7 cover instruction tuning, reward models, policy gradients and reasoning. Written by the post-training lead at the Allen Institute, so it is a practitioner account rather than a survey.

rlhfbook.com ↗

R.02

Jacob Austin and others, How To Scale Your Model

Free at jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book; Google DeepMind, 2025.

The single best source for chapter 03. Chapter 7 on transformer inference gives the arithmetic of prefill, decode and the KV cache, and chapter 8 works through the cost of serving a real model. Assumes algebra and nothing more.

jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book ↗

R.03

Epoch AI, Trends dashboard and Gradient Updates

Free at epoch.ai/trends and epochai.substack.com; updated continuously.

The best source for chapter 04. Independent, publishes its method and data, and the most reliable place to check any claim about where AI compute goes or what it costs.

epoch.ai/trends ↗

R.04

Georgia Tech CS 7643, lecture 23

Slides, Spring 2026, at faculty.cc.gatech.edu.

Covers supervised fine-tuning through to GRPO in one sitting, with the equations included. Useful if the mathematics is wanted in compact form, and short enough to read on a train.

All four are freely available online. Publication details as listed above.

A caution

A good deal of 2026 material on serving practice sits on search-optimised sites with unverifiable figures, and is best avoided for published work. If a page gives a throughput number without a batch size and a context length, it is not telling you anything.

E.05 · Ten questions

Did any of it stick?

Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere. Each answer points back to the chapter it came from.

In closing

Pretraining built the capability.
Post-training made it usable.
Inference decides who can afford it.

If a single idea survives from all five chapters, let it be that “training” is not one thing, and that the moment a question is answered is now one of the places where capability is decided.
